**Pacing findings (for the lead):**
- The Prologue meets the whole laugh clock: 6 laughs in 60 s on the ideal route (7 counting the new
  `laugh_doorframe` titter), and a first player laugh at 6.6 s and first prank at 27.7 s on the slow route.
- Dead air stays far above the 20 % target on every Night-1/2 route, even when hidden ticks are weighed as
  fast-forward. The worst gaps:
  - **Night 2's first 48 s:** Bellows spends 22 s in the hall, then 18 s in the parlour. Hush can't reach the
    hall from the kitchen window, so nothing lands before the snack at about 49 s.
  - **Night 1's required route:** the squeaker has to come from the hall, and the kettle can only be rigged
    behind his back during `pickles`, so the route waits roughly a loop and a half hidden.
  - These waits come from the GDD's own routine and geometry, so they're a design question, not a data bug
    (see the report's requests).
- The fairness fuzz (`tools/scripts/fuzz.sh all --runs=3`) is green on all three final Nights.
