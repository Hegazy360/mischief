> **Read this first — the in-game build shows the sneeze mirrored. The cause is an engine bug, not the clip.**
> In `sneeze_in_game_e2e.png`, Bellows faces **right** at the tea station and the stool flies off past the sink onto
> the terrace. The sim has him facing −1 (left) for the whole of tea and the sneeze; `probe_facing.gd` (in the task's
> work folder) checked every tick of `g0_sneeze`.
>
> **Cause:** `CharacterView._drive_rig` calls `rig.set_facing(cur.facing, true)` every frame.
> `RigPlayer.set_facing` only returns early when `_turn_t < 0`. So while a turn is still running, each call starts it
> again (`_turn_t = 0`). A standing turn never finishes, and `_fsx` stays at about **+0.72**: facing the old way and
> squashed to 72 % width.
>
> Measured with `probe_rigface.gd`: after `set_facing(-1, true)` plus `advance` for 40 frames, `_fsx` is still 0.7167.
> Prop launches take the same flipped character transform, so the stool's hop home (world −x, to x 3000) goes world
> +x instead.
>
> **Fix** (owned by `s2-rig` / `s2-view`, see the report): either `set_facing` returns when `d == _facing` whatever
> `_turn_t` is, or the view calls it only when the facing changes. With the fix, the game shows what AnimViewer shows
> below.

## Known weak spots (honest list)
- **In game the sneeze is mirrored until the facing bug is fixed** (see the top). That is not a clip problem.
- `act_tea` is subtle at phone scale. The kettle sits behind his belly at st_tea (kitchen_kettle x vs stand_x
  3000), so the pour is a gesture, not a lift.
- The strut's legs are small at phone scale. The swagger comes from the chest and the chin.
- In the sink, the slippers are only just visible over the lip.
- The two flight poses (`b_fly_a` / `b_fly_b`) differ little. The flight reads as one held diagonal.
- Contact sheets draw one stool prop per cell. In the onion's "ah" beat, two stools can look superimposed. That's a
  sheet artefact: in the game and the GIF there is only ever one.
