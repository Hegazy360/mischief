# s2-anim-hush — Phase G review (Gate 0: the Hush kitchen set + the cast sheet)

Everything here was rendered **in engine** (the game's RigPlayer in the real kitchen, AnimViewer `--room=kitchen`;
the Stalls through ViewSandbox's StallsView), at phone scale (1200 × 540 = a 20:9 phone). The 50 px silhouettes
use the Python compositor (same SVGs, alpha needed). Clips are generated by `tools/art/hush/clips_hush.py`
(`python3 tools/art/hush/build.py`); `lint_anim --rig=hush --strict`: **19 clips, 0 errors, 0 warnings** (rig lint
R04/R08 clean).

> **Known engine artefact in these captures (not the clips):** after the first smear of a part, RigPlayer leaves a
> ghost copy of the glove drawn at the feet origin (a pale glove under her shoes, below the floor line) for the rest
> of the run. Reported to `s2-rig` (see the report). Ignore it when judging the animation.

### Written rubric — the snicker (`act_snicker`, frames from `contact/act_snicker.png` / `gif/p_snicker_strip.png`)
- **Silhouette at phone size:** hunched, glove clamped over the mouth, shoulders jerking — a different shape from the
  stand (50 px silhouette 2 in `warmth.png`). Reads in half a second as "trying not to laugh".
- **Anticipation opposes the action:** f0–5 she rises (sy 1.06), chin up, gloves back, sly glance at the Stalls;
  the accent goes the other way (down and in). Lint A07 passes.
- **Accent ≤ 4 f with a pop/smear:** f6–9, `out_back` into the hunch, smear event f6 (glove).
- **Hold ≥ 30 f where the face reads:** f9–47 (38 f), shoulders at 8 Hz (keys every 3–4 f), squint + grin, a
  laugh face at f24, the head tipping further each shake (a moving hold).
- **Exactly one overshoot:** `out_back` into f9; settle f47–60 rises once (f53) then lands on the stand.
- **Sfx on the contact frame:** `hush_snicker` at f0 per `clip_events.md` (a 34-f breathy "hff-hff" that covers the
  shake). Suggestion to s2-sound: f6 (the accent) would sync the first "hff" with the snap.
- **Face swap on the accent:** glance → snicker on the f9 key. **Sliding / floor:** none (plant lock; A12/A13 clean).
- **Laugh:** none in the clip (the Stalls' titter on `rigged` is event-level).
- **Fresh?** It plays after every rig, so it will be seen ~10× a Night: Phase M adds the `act_glance` tag and should
  vary the shake (two variants). **Scores: funny 4, readable 4, fresh 3.**

### Written rubric — the dive (`act_dive`, `contact/act_dive.png` / `gif/p_dive_strip.png`)
- **Silhouette:** crouch → stretched diagonal → flat superman dive → head-first plunge → a speck: five distinct shapes
  in 15 f; the flat dive is the widest silhouette she has.
- **Anticipation opposes:** f0–3 deep crouch, arms swung back (down/back) before the up/forward launch (A07 passes).
- **Accent ≤ 4 f:** f3–6 launch, smears f1/f4 + auto smear (ghost copies — the cartoon zip).
- **Hold:** it is a 15-f sim action, not a take: the arc f6–12 is the read; she vanishes into the spot's centre
  (0, −120) on f15 exactly when the sim hides her.
- **Overshoot:** none (she is gone). **Sfx:** `dive` at f0 (its thump lands at f12 inside the sound, at the plunge).
- **Sliding / floor:** planted crouch (plant lock), no penetration (A13 clean; the beret brushes the floor line at f12).
- **Fresh?** Every hide; the cartoon suck-in is the joke. **Scores: funny 4, readable 4, fresh 3.**
