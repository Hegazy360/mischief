# Review: `s2-content` — dead air fixed at the source, timed micro-gags, the Gate-0 episode

The owner called our last game "not fun"; waiting killed it. This task retuned the slice **data** (lead decision
D1) so that something funny or interactive happens every few seconds, wrote the tiny Gate-0 scene the owner plays
first (`g0_sneeze`: ACHOO-RIA! in the kitchen), and put the dead-air gates back on every route.

## What I looked at
All 15 final timelines (`timelines/`). The grey dead-air bars are short and rare: the longest on each Night is a
**Meltdown** (N01 ≈ 36–52 s, N02 ≈ 55–70 s on the ideal routes) — Hush is hidden in the same room watching him
stomp, which the metric does not count as a reaction (request to `s2-sim`). The beat row is dense and mostly green
(room) and blue (PiP): the kitchen and parlour gags play where Hush is. N02 now opens with him reading (specs on,
the right first lesson), a squeak bit at 0.5 s, and the COLD OPEN avalanche at 19 s instead of 49 s of hiding.
N01's quiet stretch between the first Running Gag and the kettle is filled with three squeak bits (tea up his nose,
the paper ripped in half, a second tea) on the ideal route: a laugh at least every ~25 s. The Prologue timeline is
unchanged except the ⏩ lesson at 34.7 s (slow).
